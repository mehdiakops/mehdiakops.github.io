How I Work

Most Help Center problems look like writing problems and are not. An article nobody can find is an architecture problem. An article everyone finds and nobody finishes is a scoping problem. A team that keeps rewriting the same page is a governance problem.

So I spend most of my time upstream of the draft: on how content is structured, how it gets found, how quality is defined and held, and on what the data says is actually broken. I report that work to leadership in business terms rather than publishing counts.

I also stay close to the technical layer. I instrument what I own, through Pendo, Google Tag Manager, and platform APIs, and I build lightweight internal tools when the alternative is waiting a quarter for roadmap space.
